Career Record · Field Notes

Work Experience

Professional employment and training - site supervision, surveying and geotechnical fieldwork across Taplejung and neighbouring districts.

Civil Sub-Engineer

Rastriya Prasaran Grid Company Limited (RPGCL) - government sector

Mewa–Changhe 132 kV Transmission Line Project · Taplejung, Koshi Province

2022 – Present · 3+ years

  • Test-pit logging: excavated and logged every tower-footing test pit - recording soil/rock stratigraphy, weathering grade and groundwater level against the foundation design depth before approving each dig.
  • Footing inspection sign-offs: inspected each RC footing - line, level, dimensions, reinforcement cage and platform concrete - and signed off construction only after verifying compliance with RPGCL structural specifications.
  • Elevation checks: carried out dumpy-level height verification at tower platforms and footing tops, confirming design elevations and correct fall on access trails before steel erection.
  • Slope and hazard screening: evaluated rock and soil stability along the tower corridor, screened cut-slopes for landslide and erosion risk before and during excavation, and flagged mitigations to the design team.
  • Quantity and records control: tracked project quantities, maintained daily technical logs and coordinated field communication between contractors and the project office.

Mewa–Changhe alignment, Taplejung District

Ongoing since 2022

Question addressed: How do we place and verify tower foundations safely on steep, weathered Mid-Himalayan slopes?

Methodology: Walked tower alignments; identified exposed rock and soil units; logged test pits at each footing; took dumpy-level elevation checks; screened cut-slopes for landslide and erosion risk before and during excavation.

Individual engineering duties: test-pit logging at every footing (stratigraphy, weathering grade, groundwater); footing inspection sign-offs (line, level, dimensions, reinforcement, platform concrete); dumpy-level elevation verification at tower platforms; slope-hazard screening with mitigation flags to the design team; daily quantity and technical records.

My contribution: As Civil Sub-Engineer I supervised footing construction end-to-end - inspection sign-offs, quantity records, daily logs and hazard flags to the design team.

Deliverables: Verified foundations erected across the assigned stretch; continuous daily technical and quantity logs; documented hazard mitigations adopted on site.

Research & Publications

Type
Academic research (undergraduate)
Location
Rasuwagadhi–Kerung transboundary corridor, Rasuwa District
Status
Published - peer-reviewed student journal

Research question: What drives mountain flood hazards along the transboundary Trishuli-headwater corridor, and how vulnerable are the settlements and the Kodari–Rasuwagadhi trade route?

Methodology: Field visit to the corridor; channel and geomorphological observation; review of local hazard records; disaster-risk analysis of terrain vulnerability.

My contribution: Co-authored the study - field data collection, hazard mapping input and manuscript preparation.

Findings / output: Identified channel-bottleneck and glacial-lake outburst contributions to flood peaks and recommended corridor-level risk-management measures. Full citation below.
